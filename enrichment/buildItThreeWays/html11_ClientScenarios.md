# html11 Client Scenarios

Pick ONE. This client stays yours for all three builds. Every scenario has the same shape — a small organization, real content needs, and a reason they can't decide how to build their site (that's what your comparison will answer).

## 1. Riverbend Youth Soccer League

300 families. Needs: schedules (a table!), registration info with a contact form, field locations, photo gallery, weather-cancellation announcements. The league secretary — not a coder — must be able to update announcements.

## 2. The Rusty Spoon Diner

Family restaurant, 40 years old. Needs: menu with prices, hours/location, history page with old photos, contact form for party bookings. The owner wants it to "feel like us, not like a chain."

## 3. Medina Makers Club

A community workshop (3D printers, laser cutter, woodshop). Needs: equipment list with safety info, membership signup form, class calendar, project gallery. Members keep asking for a dark mode because they check it from the shop floor.

## 4. Paws & Whiskers Rescue

Animal shelter. Needs: adoptable-pet profiles (photo + details each), adoption-application form, donation info, volunteer page. New pets arrive weekly — updates must be easy.

## 5. Your own (teacher approval required)

A real club, team, business, or organization you know. Must need at minimum: multi-page structure, a data table, a form, a gallery, and regular content updates by a non-coder.
