# html11g — Handoff & Presentation

## The handoff document

Real projects end with a handoff, not a shrug. Write `html11_Handoff_lastname.md` for your client as if they were real people about to take the keys:

1. **What you're delivering** — the recommended build (from your 11f verdict), its URL/location, and what the other two builds taught you that shaped the recommendation
2. **How to update it** — three common updates (change hours, add a photo, post an announcement), each as numbered steps a non-coder can follow
3. **Support plan** — what breaks most often and what to do; where the files/accounts live; what you'd build next with another month
4. **Rollout note** — how you'd announce/launch this (when it goes live, who checks it first, the final-review step before the URL goes public)

## The presentation (5 minutes)

- The client and what they needed (30 seconds)
- All three builds, on screen (2 minutes)
- The matrix's most surprising row (1 minute)
- Your verdict and why (1 minute)
- One thing you'd tell last-August-you about web development (30 seconds)
