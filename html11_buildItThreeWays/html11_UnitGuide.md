# html11 — Build It Three Ways · Unit Guide

**The course's culminating block (replaces the old capstone). One client scenario, three builds, one professional verdict. ODE competencies: 2.9.1–2.9.6 (proposal) · 2.12.1–2.12.6 (testing & acceptance) · 2.13.1–2.13.6 (rollout & handoff) · 6.5.3, 6.5.4, 6.5.6 · plus everything the hand-coded build integrates.**

## The premise

Real web professionals don't hand-code everything — they choose between hand-coding, AI generation, and site builders based on the client, budget, and job. This block makes students do all three for the SAME client, then judge the results like professionals: with a testing checklist and a comparison matrix.
