# Build It Three Ways
## One client. Three builds. One professional verdict.

---

# The Question This Block Answers

You can now hand-code a website. So... does anyone actually do that?

**Yes — and also no.** Real developers choose between:

1. **Hand-coding** — total control, slowest
2. **AI generation** — fast, but someone has to catch its mistakes (that's you)
3. **Builders & CMSes** — fastest, least control, and the client can update it themselves

Professionals don't have a favorite. They have a **recommendation per client.**

---

# Your Client

Pick one scenario (soccer league, diner, makers club, animal rescue — or pitch your own). That client is yours for the whole block.

**First deliverable: a one-page proposal** — scope, audience, objectives, timeline, deliverables. Real projects start with paper, not code.

---

# The Three Builds

| Build | You bring | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Hand-coded | Your semester site, polished + dark mode + mobile nav | 1–2 days |
| AI-generated | Direction skills: name the components, then **critique the output** | 2 days |
| Builders | Google Sites, then WordPress — a real CMS | 1–2 days |

Then: same usability checklist on all three. No favoritism — your own build gets no free passes.

---

# The Verdict

A comparison matrix + one paragraph: **which build should YOUR client use?**

There's no right answer. There's a *well-argued* answer.

---

# Why the AI Critique Is Graded Hardest

Anyone can generate a website. Professionals can tell you **what's wrong with it**: div soup, missing alt text, fake headings, unlabeled inputs.

Spot-the-error is also exactly what your December exams ask. This is exam prep wearing a project costume.
