## Part 1 — Export-for-web images (~half the period)

Take the three heaviest images from your hand-coded site. Resize to the display size, compress, choose the right format (JPG photo / PNG sharp edges / SVG vector). Before/after file sizes recorded — connect to the html10 bandwidth slide. **Squoosh (squoosh.app) is confirmed available regardless of the Adobe answer** — side-by-side quality slider, no login, no dev tools.

## Part 2 — An SVG logo (~half the period)

A simple logo for your client — shapes and text. Export as SVG, embed it in the hand-coded site's header, then open the .svg in VS Code and look: it's markup. You can read it.

## What to commit
