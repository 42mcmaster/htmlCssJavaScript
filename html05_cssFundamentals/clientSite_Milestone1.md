## Part 1: Site Plan

Use your intake form answers to fill in `clientPlan_Lastname.md`. Copy these headings:

```markdown
# Site Plan: Business Name

## The Client
Client's name, business name, and what the business does (1–2 sentences).

## The Goal
What should a visitor do on this site? (call, visit, buy, sign up, ...)

## Pages
List the 3 to 5 web pages the client's site will have (for example: Home, About, Menu, Contact).
For each page, write one line about what goes on it. You only build the Home page this week.

## Branding
- Colors (hex codes, like #1E6FD9):
- Fonts:
- Logo: does the client have one, or does one need to be made?

## Still Needed from the Client
What text, photos, or logo files do you still need, and when will you get them?
```

---

## Part 2: Start the Home Page

Build `index.html` and `styles.css` in your `ClientSite_Lastname` folder.

**index.html must have:**

- A `<header>` with the business name
- A `<nav>` with a link to every page from your plan (the other pages don't need to exist yet)
- A `<main>` with at least two `<section>`s. Use the client's real information where you have it. Placeholder text is fine where you don't.
- A `<footer>`
- `<link rel="stylesheet" href="styles.css">` in the `<head>`

**styles.css must:**

- Use the client's colors and fonts on the `body`, `header`, `nav`, and `footer`
- Add padding and margin so the page isn't crowded (Lesson 06)

Check your page with Live Preview before you push.
